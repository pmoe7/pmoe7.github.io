AI Executive · Investor · Builder

AI strategy,made operational.

I lead AI & Platforms at Aker, a private investment firm, building at the intersection of private equity, capital markets, and enterprise technology.

My work sits where investment judgment, capital allocation, business operations, and software meet. I move between setting the enterprise roadmap and getting into the workflows, data, evaluations, and product details required to ship.

Experience

I identify decisions across underwriting, market intelligence, portfolio operations, and capital allocation where AI can create meaningful leverage, then take the work from strategy and architecture through prototyping, deployment, adoption, and measurement. The goal is not more AI. It is better judgment, faster execution, and institutional knowledge that compounds.

Before Aker, I built AI, data, and digital products at Accenture for global enterprises—including identity platforms used by millions and analytics products supporting trillions in capital. Earlier roles at Deloitte and PepsiCo gave me the finance, operating, and analytical range I now bring to AI.
